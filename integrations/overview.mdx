Sericin is designed to fit into your existing infrastructure rather than replace it. You can stream real-time events to your security and observability tools, authenticate your team through your corporate identity provider, and automate every aspect of agent governance using the REST API.

## Common integration patterns

The following patterns cover the most frequent ways teams connect Sericin to the rest of their stack:

- **Stream audit events to your SIEM** — subscribe to events like `policy.denied` and `agent.action.completed` via webhook and forward them to Splunk, Datadog, or your preferred SIEM for centralized logging and alerting.
- **Trigger pipelines from your CI/CD system** — call `POST /pipelines/{id}/run` from a GitHub Actions workflow or Jenkins pipeline to kick off governance-enforced agent tasks as part of your deployment process.
- **Provision agents programmatically via the API** — use `POST /agents` and `POST /policies` to create and configure agents as part of your infrastructure-as-code setup, keeping your agent definitions version-controlled alongside the rest of your stack.
- **Authenticate users through your corporate identity provider** — configure [SSO](/integrations/sso) so your team logs in to Sericin using the same credentials they use for every other internal tool, with role assignments driven by your existing IdP groups.
