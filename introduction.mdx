Sericin is an agent governance platform that gives your organization visibility and control over every AI agent in your system. Whether you're running a handful of task-specific agents or orchestrating large multi-agent pipelines, Sericin lets you define roles, enforce policies, route approvals, and maintain a complete audit record — all from a single control plane.

## How Sericin works

Sericin operates as the governance layer between your AI agents and the actions they take. Every agent is registered with a defined role and scope. When an agent attempts a sensitive action, Sericin evaluates the applicable policies, routes any required approvals, and records the outcome — giving you end-to-end accountability.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your agents">
    Define each agent's identity, role, and permitted action scope in the Sericin dashboard or via the API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set governance policies">
    Create policies that control what agents can do, when they can act, and what requires human approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Orchestrate pipelines">
    Connect agents into pipelines with event-driven triggers so complex workflows run automatically under governance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and audit">
    Use the audit log and monitoring dashboards to review every agent action and policy decision in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
